Maritime Records Collection

The London Maritime Archive preserves selected records relating to commercial shipping, harbour activity and vessels operating through the River Thames.

This digital catalogue contains selected documents from the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries.

Thames Cargo Register
Selected entries documenting cargo movements through London's river docks.
1906
Dockworkers' Register
Employment and shift records for dock workers operating along the Thames.
1913
Coastal Shipping Log
Arrival and departure records for vessels operating between British coastal ports.

River Transport Survey
Survey notes concerning commercial river transportation following the war.
