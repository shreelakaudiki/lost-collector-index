RECORD VS-1882-17
12 December 1882 · London

Winter Charity Exhibition

The Society's winter exhibition was held in December for the purpose of raising funds for several local charitable institutions.

The exhibition included displays of embroidery, domestic crafts, decorative objects and illustrated books. Members of the Society contributed several privately owned pieces for public viewing.

Attendance was recorded as satisfactory, with additional donations received during the final afternoon of the exhibition.

The organising committee thanked all participating members and recommended that a similar exhibition be arranged the following winter.
