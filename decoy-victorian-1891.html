6 May 1891 · London

Exhibition of Domestic Arts

The Society's spring exhibition presented examples of domestic craftsmanship contributed by members and local exhibitors.

Particular attention was given to embroidery, decorative needlework and household textiles. Several demonstrations were held during the opening week.

SELECTED EXHIBITION CATEGORIES
Embroidered Household Linen
Decorative Needlework
Traditional Textile Patterns
Domestic Woodwork
Illustrated Household Manuals

The exhibition remained open to Society members throughout the month. The committee recorded favourable attendance and several new donations to the Society's domestic arts collection.

ARCHIVE NOTE

Catalogue preserved in the Society's exhibitions collection. The original exhibition programme remains incomplete.
