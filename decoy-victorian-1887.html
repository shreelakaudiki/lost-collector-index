RECORD VS-1887-09
21 November 1887 · London

Annual Society Dinner

The annual dinner of the Victorian Society was held at the Society's usual meeting rooms in November. Members and invited guests were received from seven o'clock in the evening.

The dinner was followed by speeches concerning the Society's activities during the preceding year and plans for the forthcoming season.

REGISTERED GUESTS
Mrs. Margaret Bell
Mr. Charles Harrington
Miss Edith Moore
Mr. William Cartwright
Mrs. Eleanor Finch
Rev. Thomas Wilkes

The evening concluded shortly before midnight. The committee recorded its thanks to the members responsible for arranging the event.

ARCHIVE NOTE

Register preserved as part of the Society's annual meetings collection.
